Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Morse Shikho does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your lesson progress stays on your own device.

Who we are

Morse Shikho is made by Ikshana Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Morse Shikho app for iPhone and the website at morse-shikho.vercel.app.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Which letters you have unlocked
  • Your practice results for each letter
  • How many answers you gave each day, used for your daily goal and streak
  • Your settings, such as speed, tone pitch, volume and daily goal

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by deleting the app.

Fonts

The app displays text using fonts from Google Fonts. When the app loads, your device may request these font files from Google's servers, which, like any web request, shares your IP address with Google. No other information is sent. Google's handling of this is described in the Google Privacy Policy. If your device is offline, the app still works using built-in system fonts.

Tracking, ads and analytics

Morse Shikho does not track you across apps or websites, does not show advertising and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
